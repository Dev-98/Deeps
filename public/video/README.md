# The video goes here

Name it exactly:

    one-more-thing.mp4

and it appears in chapter 10 automatically. Optionally add
`poster.jpg` — the still frame she sees before pressing play.

- **`.mp4` (H.264 + AAC).** Other formats are a gamble on her phone.
- **Shoot it vertical.** She'll watch it on a phone. The frame is 9:16.
- **Under ~50MB.** Two minutes at 1080p is usually fine. Any bigger and
  the loading spinner becomes part of the memory.
- **It never autoplays.** She presses it — a browser rule, and the right
  call for something she might open in public.

Until the file exists the chapter still works: it shows a placeholder
saying the video isn't there yet, so you can test the whole flow before
you've filmed anything.
